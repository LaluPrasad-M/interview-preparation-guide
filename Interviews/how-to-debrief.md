# Interview Debriefs

One file per company: `Interviews/<company>.md`. Write it within an hour, while
you still remember the exact wording.

Keep it to what *you* experienced. Do not paste verbatim question banks from
sources you were asked to keep confidential, and keep recruiter names and
anything internal out of a repo other people can clone.

## Template

```markdown
# <Company>

- Role / round:
- Date:
- Format: (DSA / system design / behavioural / mixed)

## Asked

1.

## What I got wrong

## What to revise

```
