# IaaS, CaaS and FaaS

> [!tldr]
> The cloud service ladder: IaaS hands you a raw server, CaaS a place to run containers, FaaS just a function, with less of your own management at each step.

Infrastructure as a Service, EC2, gives you the machine: OS, networking and scaling are your problem. Container as a Service, ECS or EKS, takes the OS off your plate and runs your containers. Function as a Service, Lambda, takes the container off your plate too; you hand over only the code.

The three are the same AWS compute capability at different levels of abstraction, not different products for different jobs.
