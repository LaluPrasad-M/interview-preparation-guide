# Questions To Ask Them

Keep three ready per round type. Specific beats generic.

## Engineer / peer round

- What does the path from merged PR to production look like?
- What breaks most often, and who gets paged?

## Manager round

- How is scope decided for someone at my level in the first six months?
- What separates someone doing well here from someone struggling?
